Frequently Asked Questions — Software Development in Lebanon

1. Is Lebanon a good destination for software outsourcing?
Yes, Lebanese developers are highly educated, multilingual (English/French/Arabic), and creative. Rates are competitive compared to Europe.
2. Which cities have the most IT companies?
Beirut is the primary hub, followed by Jounieh, Tripoli, Saida, and Dbayeh.
3. What technologies do Lebanese software firms excel in?
Fintech, blockchain, mobile apps (iOS/Android), web development (React, Angular), and cloud solutions.
4. How reliable is the internet infrastructure for remote work?
Major cities have fiber optics and 4G/5G, many firms operate hybrid/remote seamlessly.
5. Are Lebanese software companies familiar with agile?
Absolutely, most follow Scrum and Kanban, delivering iterative value.
6. Do they have experience with international clients?
Yes, many firms serve clients in UAE, KSA, Europe, and the USA.
7. What is the typical engagement model?
Dedicated teams, fixed-price projects, and staff augmentation are common.
8. How to evaluate a Lebanese software company?
Check portfolios, Clutch ratings, case studies, and client testimonials.
9. Are data protection laws respected?
While Lebanon has data protection laws in progress, reputable companies follow GDPR-like standards for international clients.
