Libya has a young, educated workforce with competitive rates. However, political stability varies; major cities like Tripoli and Misrata are relatively stable tech hubs.

Tripoli is the largest hub, followed by Benghazi, Misrata, and Sabha.
3. What technologies do Libyan developers specialize in?
Web (PHP, .NET, React), mobile (Android/Kotlin, Flutter), fintech, and industrial automation.
