Frequently Asked Questions — Software Development in Lesotho

1. Is Lesotho an emerging tech hub?
Yes, Maseru is growing with government support for digital transformation, and local talent is increasing through coding bootcamps.
2. Which cities have software companies?
Maseru is the primary hub, followed by Teyateyaneng, Leribe, and Maputsoe.
3. What technologies are popular among Lesotho developers?
Web (PHP, Laravel, React), mobile (Flutter, Kotlin), and cloud (AWS, Azure) are common.
4. Do Lesotho software firms serve international clients?
Some do, especially those focused on fintech and agri-tech, but most serve local and South African markets.
5. What is the typical hourly rate for developers?
Rates are very competitive, typically $20–$40 per hour, lower than South Africa.
6. Are there software companies specializing in government systems?
Yes, InfoTech and NTT have strong public sector portfolios.
7. How reliable is internet connectivity?
Maseru has fiber and LTE; remote work is feasible with backup connections.
8. Can I hire a dedicated team from Lesotho?
Yes, several firms offer staff augmentation and dedicated development teams.
9. Do they follow agile methodologies?
Most modern firms use Scrum or Kanban for project management.
